About

## About Me I’m Dr. Harshavardhan, a final-year MD Pharmacology resident at AIIMS Bibinagar, with a growing focus on Medical Affairs, Clinical Pharmacology, and evidence-driven pharmaceutical medicine. My journey has been shaped by a simple interest: understanding how clinical evidence can move beyond the pages of a journal and translate into better decisions in real-world healthcare. Through my academic training and industry experiences, I have developed an interdisciplinary perspective spanning clinical research, pharmacovigilance, regulatory science, medical affairs, and evidence synthesis. My industry exposure includes experience with Medical Affairs and pharmacovigilance workflows at Hetero Drugs Ltd., followed by selection for the AstraZeneca PHARMASTEER Medical Affairs Internship, a nationally competitive program. These experiences have given me an appreciation of how scientific evidence is evaluated, communicated, and translated into medical strategy within the pharmaceutical industry. Alongside industry exposure, my academic work has focused on antimicrobial resistance, antimicrobial pharmacology, and evidence synthesis, including systematic reviews and meta-analyses. I have worked with clinical datasets, MIC-based antimicrobial susceptibility data, guideline interpretation, and structured approaches to evaluating clinical evidence. I have also pursued formal training in Good Clinical Practice, pharmacovigilance, Medical Affairs, pharmaceutical development, artificial intelligence in healthcare, and biomedical research, complemented by the McKinsey Forward Program and the CPD P2PC certification. What drives me I’m particularly interested in roles where scientific rigor meets strategic thinking — translating complex clinical evidence into clear, credible, and actionable insights for healthcare professionals, patients, and cross-functional teams. My long-term goal is to build a career as a Medical Affairs professional, contributing to evidence generation, scientific communication, medical strategy, and responsible development and use of medicines. Clinical evidence. Scientific curiosity. Strategic thinking. That’s the intersection I’m building my career around.
